AIVILO Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

AIVILO is a free browser game. This page explains what the game and its game server keep about you, why, and for how long. We keep as little as the game needs, we never sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

If you play AIVILO on CrazyGames.com, CrazyGames' own privacy policy covers their site, their account and their ads. There the game has no account of its own: your progress is saved by CrazyGames (to your CrazyGames account when you are logged in, otherwise in your browser), and the name other players see is your CrazyGames username, or a Guest_ name if you are not logged in. This page covers the game itself and its game server.

What we keep

WhatWhyHow long
Your player name and your IP address, when you play onlineTo keep cheaters and abusive players out of the game (bans)90 days, then deleted automatically
If you create an account (outside CrazyGames): your username, email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your passwordSo you can log in and keep your progress on any deviceUntil you delete the account
If you have an account: your saved progress (rank, unlocks, loadouts, challenges, settings)To give you your progress when you log inUntil you delete the account
Your player name and results in online rounds (score, kills, deaths, wins)The public leaderboardsWhile you are among the board's top 2,000 players
Reports made with the in-game Report button: the names involved and the reasonTo deal with cheating and abuseIn the server's log, until it is cleared

The game server also keeps nightly backups of this data for up to 30 days, in private storage, to recover from a failure. A deleted account disappears from the backups as they expire.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings and progress in your browser's storage (local storage) so you can carry on where you left off. It is not a tracking cookie and it never leaves your device unless you log in to an account. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Other services

The game's page loads the CrazyGames SDK from crazygames.com, which the game uses for ads and accounts when it runs on CrazyGames. Outside CrazyGames the game does not use it, but your browser still downloads it from their servers. The game uses no other analytics, trackers or advertising services.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest. Accounts are optional; every mode and map can be played without one.
  • Delete your account (outside CrazyGames) at any time: in the game, open your account (Account on the main menu), choose Delete account and confirm with your password. Your username, email, password and saved progress are deleted from the game server at once.
  • Play offline (Play offline on the main menu): nothing about you is sent to the game server.
  • Ask us what we keep about you, or to delete it sooner (your name and IP address records, a leaderboard entry, an account): email Aivilo.Support@gmail.com with your player name or username. On CrazyGames, your CrazyGames account and the progress saved in it are managed by CrazyGames.

Children

AIVILO is not made for children under 13. If you are under 13 (16 in the European Union), please do not create an account without a parent's permission. You can still play as a guest.

Security

Connections to the game server are encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, never as the password itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
